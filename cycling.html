Cycling

Samobor is also known for its bicycle trails organized and marked by numbers from 1 to 4. Highlands of Samobor is an excellent location for mountain biking, while on the way back one can stop and rest having a cup of coffee and a slice of cremeschnitte. One of the most well known trails are: Fairy Trail (Grdanjci - Medven Draga), The Waterfall Trail (Medven Draga - Grdanjci), The Trail of the Bats (Samobor – Koretići - Samobor) and The Trail of St. Gera (Ozalj Castle - Sošice).
