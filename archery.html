Archery

Citizens of Samobor and visitors of this beautiful small town got the chance to enjoy yet another sport – archery. Archery Club Samobor was founded ten years ago and is located in the former barracks Taborec, in the hall in Bregana, and in the facilities of Primary School Bogumil Toni. The archery club welcomes athletes of all ages, who may participate in national and international competitions.
