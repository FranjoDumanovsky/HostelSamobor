Events

What's on in Samobor

An overview of the most important events in the town of Samobor and its surroundings.

End of February

Samobor Carnival

The most well known event in the town of Samobor – the Carnival takes place every year end of February. It is one of the oldest and biggest carnival celebrations in the continental part of Croatia.

View more

March 1st

Battle of Samobor

Every year on the 1st of March the Vungriščak park turns into a knight's military camp with horseman, swordsmen, armored knights, battle machines and catapults.

View more

First weekend of July

Rudarska greblica — Miners Cake's Days

The Miners Cake's Days are traditionally held on the first weekend of July. The event is dedicated to the traditional miners cake called Rudarska greblica.

View more

Third weekend of October

Day of the Town of Samobor

Day of the Town of Samobor is marked on the third weekend in October, and lasts for four days. In 1242 King Béla IV of Hungary made Samobor a free royal town.

View more

International event

Samobor Salami Day

The making of salami, sausages, and other cured meat products is a big tradition in Samobor, cherished by every family in this beautiful small town.

View more

Activities

Things to do around Samobor

Find out everything you can do in Samobor and its surroundings.

Hiking

If one is a big fan of hiking and loves exploring the nature Samobor is a right place for that. The hills of Samobor and Žumberak have interwoven network of walking and hiking marked trails.

View more

Cycling

Samobor is also known for its bicycle trails organized and marked by numbers from 1 to 4. Highlands of Samobor is an excellent location for mountain biking.

View more

Horseback riding

If you are a big fan of horseback riding our little town offers that activity as well. Molvice valley is the right place for you — no foreknowledge about horses needed, just the will to try.

View more

Motorcycle tours

Indescribable beauty is hidden in the town of Samobor. This little town can be experienced and explored in a number of ways, including the motorcycle tours.

View more

Archery

Citizens of Samobor and visitors of this beautiful small town got the chance to enjoy yet another sport – archery. Archery Club Samobor was founded ten years ago.
