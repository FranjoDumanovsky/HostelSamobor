Hiking

If one is a big fan of hiking and loves exploring the nature Samobor is a right place for that. The hills of Samobor and Žumberak have interwoven network of walking and hiking marked trails. Oštrc is one of the highest peaks of the hills surrounding Samobor and is located only 30 km from the Zagreb city centre. Ascent is very easy and part of the way is accessible by car. Japetić, the highest peak of the highlands of Samobor, stands at 879 m above sea level and is a favourite hiking destination. At the very top there is a high pyramid set up in 1960. The area of Okić is situated in the southeastern part of the Samobor highlands, and the main point of this area is Okić itself. On the cliffs of the hills was built the Old Burg. Make sure to visit the Ethno House under Okić. Hike and rock climbing around the Old Burg begins at the mountain lodge Maks Plotnikov.
