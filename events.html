Events

What's on in Samobor

An overview of the most important events in the town of Samobor and its surroundings.

End of February

Samobor Carnival

The most well known event in the town of Samobor – the Carnival takes place every year end of February. It is one of the oldest and biggest carnival celebrations in the continental part of Croatia.

View more

March 1st

Battle of Samobor

Every year on the 1st of March the Vungriščak park turns into a knight's military camp with horseman, swordsmen, armored knights, battle machines and catapults.

View more

First weekend of July

Rudarska greblica — Miners Cake's Days

The Miners Cake's Days are traditionally held on the first weekend of July. The event is dedicated to the traditional miners cake called Rudarska greblica.

View more

Third weekend of October

Day of the Town of Samobor

Day of the Town of Samobor is marked on the third weekend in October, and lasts for four days. In 1242 King Béla IV of Hungary made Samobor a free royal town.

View more

International event

Samobor Salami Day

The making of salami, sausages, and other cured meat products is a big tradition in Samobor, cherished by every family in this beautiful small town.
