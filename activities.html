Things to do around Samobor

Find out everything you can do in Samobor and its surroundings.

Hiking

If one is a big fan of hiking and loves exploring the nature Samobor is a right place for that. The hills of Samobor and Žumberak have interwoven network of walking and hiking marked trails.

View more

Cycling

Samobor is also known for its bicycle trails organized and marked by numbers from 1 to 4. Highlands of Samobor is an excellent location for mountain biking.

View more

Horseback riding

If you are a big fan of horseback riding our little town offers that activity as well. Molvice valley is the right place for you — no foreknowledge about horses needed, just the will to try.

View more

Motorcycle tours

Indescribable beauty is hidden in the town of Samobor. This little town can be experienced and explored in a number of ways, including the motorcycle tours.

View more

Archery

Citizens of Samobor and visitors of this beautiful small town got the chance to enjoy yet another sport – archery. Archery Club Samobor was founded ten years ago.
