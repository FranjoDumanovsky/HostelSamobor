Horseback riding

If you are a big fan of horseback riding our little town offers that activity as well. If you want to get away from the city and spend some time in the nature with animals, Molvice valley is the right place for you. You don't need any foreknowledge about horses, just the will to try. There you can enjoy the beautiful horses, views, meadows and general peace and quiet. Molvice are located close to Samobor, about 12 minutes drive from the Jankomir Bridge. All horses are quiet and trained for beginners, but you can also have a riding instructor accompany you if you need one.
